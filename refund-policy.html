Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains the refund and cancellation rules for DreamCash services.

1. Activation Fee

The ₹99 activation fee is charged for activating your DreamCash account. Once your account is successfully activated, this fee is generally non-refundable.

2. Failed Activation

If your payment is successful but your account is not activated due to a verified technical issue, you may contact DreamCash Support. After verification, appropriate action will be taken.

3. Duplicate Payment

If the same payment is made more than once by mistake, please contact our support team with your payment details. After verification, the extra payment may be refunded or adjusted.

4. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, it may take approximately 5–7 working days depending on the payment provider and banking system.

5. Contact Us

For any refund-related queries, please contact DreamCash Support using the Contact Us page available on this website.
